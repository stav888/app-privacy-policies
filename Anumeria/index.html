Anumeria

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026

Anumeria calculates numerology results on your device. The app does not send your profile details to its developer or to analytics, advertising, or other third-party services.

1. Who operates Anumeria

Anumeria is operated by Stazi. For privacy questions, contact stavk789@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

Depending on the features you use, Anumeria stores the following information locally:

  • The name you enter, including optional middle and last names.
  • Your date of birth, used to calculate numerology results.
  • Up to six recent name and date-of-birth entries for the Recent logins autofill feature.
  • Your app preferences, such as theme, accent colour, and calculation mode.
  • Whether you enabled the daily reminder.

The app uses Android WebView local storage for profile, history, and display preferences, and Android app preferences for reminder state. These details are used only to provide the app features you choose.

3. How information is used

Your name and date of birth are processed on your device to calculate and display numerology results. Preferences control how the app looks. If enabled, a local Android alarm schedules a notification for 7:00 a.m. in the device's current time zone. The notification contains a general reminder message, not your name or date of birth.

4. Sharing and network access

Anumeria has no internet permission and makes no network requests. It has no account system, analytics, advertising, crash-reporting service, third-party SDK, or payment provider. The developer does not receive your profile data, and the app does not sell or share it.

When you enable reminders, Android schedules and displays the notification locally. Anumeria requests notification and exact-alarm access for that feature, and receives the Android boot event so it can reschedule an enabled reminder after a restart. It does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photos.

5. Retention and deletion

Your active profile and preferences remain on the device until you delete them, clear Anumeria's app data, or uninstall the app. Recent logins are limited to six entries. In the app, choose Settings, then Danger zone, then Delete everything to remove the saved profile, preferences, and login history. Delete profile removes the active profile but keeps Recent logins.

Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for Anumeria's app data. If you use the standalone browser version, its data remains in that browser's local storage until you use Delete everything or clear the browser's site data.

6. Security

Android keeps app data within the app's private storage area, and Anumeria excludes app data from Android backup and device transfer. The app does not separately encrypt the profile data it stores. Anyone who can unlock or otherwise access your device may be able to access that data, so use your device's screen lock and do not share an unlocked device.

7. Children

Anumeria is a general-purpose numerology app and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly receive information about children; information entered into the app remains on the device.

8. Your choices and rights

You can review and correct information in the app, delete your profile or all saved data, or clear the app's data in Android settings. Depending on where you live, local privacy laws may give you additional rights. Because the developer does not receive or hold your profile data, requests to access or delete that data can be completed on your device.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, its effective date will be updated. Material changes will also be identified in the app.
